Community Assets & Partners

Connecting schools with WA organizations supporting CALD communities

Building Partnerships for Cultural Safety

Western Australia is home to numerous organizations dedicated to supporting culturally and linguistically diverse communities. These community assets provide essential services ranging from mental health support and settlement assistance to cultural celebration and youth programs. Schools can partner with these organizations to strengthen cultural safety, enhance student wellbeing, and build meaningful connections with CALD families.

🧠 Mental Health & Youth Services

Free and confidential mental health support for young people aged 12-25, including counseling, crisis support, and wellbeing services.

Free counseling and wellbeing services for young people aged 12-25. Walk-in appointments available, no mental health care plan required.

Services include:
