Creating Culturally Safe Schools

Promoting respect, inclusion, and belonging for every student and community.

Cultural safety in schools means more than celebrating diversity. It's about ensuring that every student, staff member, and family feels safe, respected, and valued — where identity and culture are not only acknowledged but protected.

Our mission is to make it easier for educators and schools to build cultural competence through access to practical, evidence-based, and free resources.
