About This Project

Building cultural safety and competence in education

This website was created to promote cultural safety and cultural competence in school communities. We believe that education can be a powerful space for reconciliation, respect, and belonging.

Cultural Safety

Cultural safety means that everyone — students, staff, and families — can express who they are without fear of discrimination or judgment.

Cultural Competence

Cultural competence means that educators and schools have the knowledge, skills, and reflection needed to work respectfully across cultures.

Why This Matters

Schools shape how young people see themselves and others.

When schools are culturally safe:

  • Students feel confident and proud of their identity
  • Families are more engaged and connected
  • Teachers can create more inclusive and effective learning environments
  • The whole community benefits from greater understanding and respect

Building cultural competence helps reduce bias and racism, supports wellbeing, and strengthens relationships between schools and communities.

Our Goals

  • Share practical, open-access resources for teachers, school leaders, and community partners
  • Support reflection and growth through self-assessment and professional learning tools
  • Highlight examples of good practice from schools across Australia and internationally
  • Encourage collaboration between educators, students, and families

Who We Are

This site is a collaborative project developed by students and educators passionate about inclusive and culturally responsive education. We aim to make it easier for teachers and schools to find, share, and apply tools that build safe, respectful, and empowering learning environments.
